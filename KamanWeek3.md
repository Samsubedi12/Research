# Literature Review: Artificial Intelligence in Cybersecurity

## Paper 1

### Title
**Artificial Intelligence and Cybersecurity: Challenges and Opportunities**

### Source
Springer (2024)

### Link
https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s43681-024-00427-4

### Key Insights
This paper discusses the increasing importance of Artificial Intelligence (AI) in cybersecurity and its impact on improving cyber defence mechanisms. The study highlights that machine learning algorithms are more effective than traditional security approaches in analysing large volumes of security data, detecting abnormal patterns, and identifying potential cyber threats.

The paper identifies several applications of AI in cybersecurity:

- Automated threat detection and response.
- Malware and intrusion detection.
- Behaviour analysis for detecting abnormal user activities.
- Predictive security analytics to prevent future attacks.

### Methodology
The researchers conducted a literature analysis by examining existing studies related to AI and cybersecurity. The study reviewed current applications, benefits, limitations, and future challenges of AI-based cybersecurity solutions.

### Key Findings
- AI improves the speed and accuracy of cyber threat detection.
- Machine learning models can identify previously unknown attack patterns.
- AI automates cybersecurity tasks and reduces workload for security professionals.
- Attackers can also use AI technologies to develop more advanced cyberattacks.

### Research Gap
The paper identifies several challenges:

- Lack of transparency in AI decision-making, known as the black-box problem.
- Requirement for high-quality and reliable training data.
- Risks of malicious attacks targeting AI systems.

### Contribution to Research Topic
This paper provides a broad understanding of how AI can enhance cybersecurity while highlighting the risks and limitations associated with relying on AI-driven security tools.

# Paper 2

## Title
**Mitigating Cybersecurity Challenges in the Financial Sector with Artificial Intelligence**

### Source
Ulster University Research Repository

### Link
https://pure.ulster.ac.uk/en/publications/mitigating-cybersecurity-challenges-in-the-financial-sector-with-/

### Key Insights
This research focuses on applying AI technologies to protect financial organisations from cybersecurity threats. Since financial institutions manage valuable customer data and transaction information, they are common targets for cybercriminals.

The paper discusses AI applications including:

- Machine learning algorithms for fraud detection.
- Real-time transaction monitoring.
- Identification of unusual activities.
- Automated incident response systems.

### Methodology
The study uses a review and analysis approach to evaluate existing AI-based cybersecurity solutions implemented in financial organisations. It examines how AI technologies address security challenges within banking and financial environments.

### Key Findings
- AI improves fraud detection by analysing transaction patterns.
- Machine learning models quickly identify abnormal financial behaviour.
- AI enables faster cybersecurity incident response.
- Predictive analytics helps organisations prevent attacks before major damage occurs.

### Research Gap
The study highlights several unresolved issues:

- Challenges in balancing security requirements with customer privacy.
- AI systems require continuous updates to remain effective.
- Organisations need skilled professionals to manage AI-based security systems.

### Contribution to Research Topic
This paper demonstrates a practical application of AI in cybersecurity by showing how machine learning technologies protect sensitive financial systems from cyber threats.

# Paper 3

## Title
**Impact of Artificial Intelligence on Cybersecurity: Investigating AI and Machine Learning for Threat Detection and Cyber Attack Mitigation**

### Source
Google Books Academic Chapter

### Link
https://books.google.com.au/books?id=l5jiEAAAQBAJ

### Key Insights
This paper explores the relationship between AI, machine learning, and modern cybersecurity practices. It explains that traditional cybersecurity approaches are becoming less effective due to increasingly complex cyber threats.

AI provides advanced capabilities that allow security systems to learn from previous attacks and improve threat detection.

The study discusses AI applications such as:

- Machine learning-based intrusion detection systems.
- AI-powered malware classification.
- Automated vulnerability identification.
- Security intelligence and threat prediction.

### Methodology
The research follows a conceptual review approach by analysing previous studies related to AI applications in cybersecurity. It compares traditional cybersecurity methods with AI-enhanced security approaches.

### Key Findings
- AI improves cybersecurity efficiency through rapid analysis of large datasets.
- Machine learning identifies patterns associated with cyber attacks.
- AI systems provide faster detection and automated responses.
- AI supports cybersecurity professionals through intelligent recommendations.

### Research Gap
The paper identifies several challenges:

- AI models may produce incorrect predictions when trained with poor-quality data.
- Attackers can manipulate AI systems using adversarial techniques.
- Ethical concerns exist regarding automated cybersecurity decisions.

### Contribution to Research Topic
This paper provides theoretical knowledge about AI-driven cybersecurity approaches and explains how AI transforms modern threat detection and cyber attack prevention.

# Common Themes Across All Three Papers

| Common Theme | Explanation |
|--------------|-------------|
| AI-based threat detection | All papers highlight that AI and machine learning improve the speed and accuracy of identifying cyber threats. |
| Automation in cybersecurity | AI reduces manual security tasks by automating monitoring, detection, and response processes. |
| Predictive security | Machine learning helps predict potential cyber attacks before they occur. |
| Challenges of AI adoption | All studies discuss limitations including data quality, privacy concerns, transparency issues, and adversarial attacks. |
| AI as both defence and threat | AI strengthens cybersecurity but can also be misused by attackers to create sophisticated attacks. |


---

# Overall Research Gap

Although AI provides significant improvements in cybersecurity, several challenges require further investigation:

- AI systems require large and accurate datasets for effective performance.
- Many AI models lack explainability, making decision-making processes difficult to understand.
- Cyber attackers may exploit AI technologies to develop advanced attack techniques.
- Further research is needed to develop ethical, secure, and transparent AI-based cybersecurity frameworks.
