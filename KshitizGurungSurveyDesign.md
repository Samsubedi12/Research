Survey Design

Research Topic

Impact of Artificial Intelligence on Cybersecurity

1. Objectives of the Survey

The survey aims to understand participants’ awareness and perceptions of how artificial intelligence (AI) and machine learning (ML) are used in cybersecurity. It will examine perceived effectiveness in threat detection and attack prevention, common concerns about AI-based security tools, trust in AI-supported cybersecurity decisions, and expectations about the future role of AI in protecting digital systems.

2. How the Survey Will Be Conducted and Distributed

The survey will be administered online using Google Forms or Microsoft Forms. A link will be distributed through university student channels, group networks, email, and suitable social media or class communication platforms. The online format will make participation convenient and will automatically organize responses for later quantitative and qualitative analysis.

3. Selected Sample for the Survey

The expected sample size is approximately 30–50 participants. The target participants will mainly be university students and adults who use digital technologies, with preference for participants who have some awareness of IT, cybersecurity, or AI. Participants should be 18 years or older and participation will be voluntary.

5. Ethical Considerations

Before starting the survey, participants will receive a short information statement explaining the purpose of the research and that participation is voluntary. No unnecessary personally identifiable information will be requested. Responses will be kept confidential and reported in summarized form. Participants may stop before submitting the survey, and the questions will avoid harmful or unnecessarily sensitive content. Survey data will be stored securely, used only for the academic research project, and accessed only by the research group where appropriate.
