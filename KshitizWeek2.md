# Section C – Research Question and Hypothesis

## Research Topic
**Impact of Artificial Intelligence on Cybersecurity:** Investigate how Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning are used to enhance cybersecurity measures, detect threats, and mitigate cyber attacks.

## 1. Research Question
How do Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning improve cybersecurity by enhancing threat detection and mitigating cyber attacks?

## 2. Hypothesis
The use of Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning significantly improves cybersecurity by increasing the accuracy of threat detection and reducing the impact of cyber attacks.

## 3. Evaluation

### Research Question: Good
**Reasons:**
- It is focused and specific, clearly identifying the use of Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning in cybersecurity.
- It is relevant to the research topic and addresses an important issue in cybersecurity.
- It is feasible to investigate through data collection and existing research.
- It is significant because it contributes to understanding how AI and Machine Learning enhance cybersecurity.

### Hypothesis: Good
**Reasons:**
- It is specific and clearly predicts the relationship between AI/Machine Learning and cybersecurity.
- It is testable through empirical research and data analysis.
- It is directly related to the research question and aligns with the objectives of the study.
