# Paper 1

## Title
**A Survey on the Role of Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning and Deep Learning for Cybersecurity Attack Detection**

## Source
IEEE Xplore

## Summary
This survey provides an overview of how Artificial Intelligence (AI), Machine Learning (ML), and Deep Learning (DL) are used in cybersecurity. It discusses different attack detection techniques, including malware detection, intrusion detection, phishing detection, and anomaly detection. The paper also compares traditional security approaches with AI-based techniques and explains how intelligent systems improve threat detection accuracy. :contentReference[oaicite:0]{index=0}

### Key Findings
- AI improves cyber attack detection accuracy.
- Machine Learning enables faster identification of unknown threats.
- Deep Learning performs well on complex cybersecurity datasets.
- AI-based approaches outperform many traditional signature-based detection methods. :contentReference[oaicite:1]{index=1}

### Methodology
- Survey and comparative analysis of existing AI, ML, and DL techniques.

### Contribution
Provides a strong theoretical foundation for understanding AI-driven cybersecurity.

### Research Gap
- High computational cost.
- Limited explainability of AI models.
- Need for more robust real-time detection systems. :contentReference[oaicite:2]{index=2}

# Common Themes

- Artificial Intelligence improves cybersecurity through automation.
- Machine Learning enhances threat and intrusion detection.
- AI helps reduce response time to cyber attacks.
- Intelligent systems detect both known and unknown threats more effectively than traditional methods.

---

# Research Gaps

The reviewed papers identify several common research gaps:

- Improving explainable AI for cybersecurity.
- Detecting sophisticated zero-day attacks.
- Reducing false positives.
- Developing lightweight AI models for real-time environments.
- Building adaptive Machine Learning models that continuously learn from new threats.

---

# Relevance to Our Research

These papers directly support our research question:

**Research Question**

> How do Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning improve cybersecurity by enhancing threat detection and mitigating cyber attacks?

The literature shows that AI and Machine Learning improve cybersecurity by increasing detection accuracy, automating threat analysis, reducing response time, and strengthening intrusion detection systems. However, challenges such as explainability, model adaptability, and zero-day attack detection remain important areas for future research.
