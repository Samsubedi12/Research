# Literature Review

## Research Topic

**Impact of Artificial Intelligence on Cybersecurity: Investigate how Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning are used to enhance cybersecurity measures, detect threats, and mitigate cyber attacks.**

---

# Paper 1

## Title

**A Survey on Machine Learning Techniques for Cyber Security in the Last Decade**

## Source

IEEE Xplore – IEEE Access

## Summary

This paper gives a comprehensive overview of the Machine Learning Techniques in Cybersecurity. It covers topics like intrusion detection, malware detection, spam detection, and computer/mobile network security using Machine Learning. Commonly used Cyber security datasets, Machine Learning methods, evaluation metrics and tools are also discussed. It has brought to the fore the potential of ML to reinforce cybersecurity, and also the issues surrounding trustworthiness of and safety of Machine Learning systems.

### Key Findings

* Machine Learning plays an important role in detecting cybersecurity threats.
* ML techniques are useful for intrusion detection, malware detection, and spam detection.
* Machine Learning can help identify attacks that conventional security approaches may fail to detect.
* The performance of ML cybersecurity systems depends on the algorithms, datasets, and evaluation methods used.
* Machine Learning systems themselves can be vulnerable to attacks by malicious adversaries.

### Methodology

* Comprehensive survey and comparative review of Machine Learning techniques used in cybersecurity research during the previous decade.
* Analysis of commonly used cybersecurity datasets, ML methods, tools, and evaluation metrics.

### Contribution

Provides a broad overview of the development and application of Machine Learning in cybersecurity and identifies important trends, techniques, datasets, and challenges associated with ML-based cybersecurity systems.

### Research Gap

* Trustworthiness of Machine Learning systems remains a major concern.
* ML systems can be vulnerable to adversarial attacks.
* More robust methods are required to protect ML-based cybersecurity systems.
* Further research is required to improve the effectiveness of ML against evolving cyber threats.

# Paper 2

## Title

**Machine Learning and Deep Learning Methods for Cybersecurity**

## Source

IEEE Xplore – IEEE Access

## Summary

In this paper, we discuss the use of Machine Learning and Deep Learning techniques in Cybersecurity, especially in Network Intrusion Detection. The authors address a variety of ML/ DL methods and provide explanations on how they may be applied to detect malicious network activities. The study also explores some of the most popular cybersecurity databases and the problems that arise when attempting to use intelligent methods in the face of a constantly evolving cyber attack.

### Key Findings

* Machine Learning and Deep Learning can improve network intrusion detection.
* Deep Learning can automatically learn useful features from complex cybersecurity data.
* Different ML and DL algorithms provide different advantages depending on the cybersecurity problem.
* The quality and characteristics of cybersecurity datasets have an important influence on detection performance.
* AI-based approaches provide opportunities to improve detection of increasingly complex cyber attacks.

### Methodology

* Survey and review of existing Machine Learning and Deep Learning methods used for cybersecurity and intrusion detection.
* Review of commonly used network security datasets and previous research applying different ML and DL techniques.

### Contribution

Provides an overview of major Machine Learning and Deep Learning approaches for cybersecurity and helps researchers understand their applications in network intrusion detection.

### Research Gap

* Cyber attacks continue to change rapidly, creating challenges for existing detection models.
* Cybersecurity datasets need to accurately represent modern network attacks.
* Further research is needed to improve the effectiveness and reliability of ML and DL approaches.
* More effective methods are needed to handle complex and evolving cybersecurity threats.

# Paper 3

## Title

**Artificial Intelligence for Cybersecurity: A Systematic Mapping of Literature**

## Source

IEEE Xplore – IEEE Access

## Summary
This paper provides a systematic review of the current literature to understand the use of AI in cybersecurity. The authors clarify that as cybercrime grows more complex, it is necessary to have more advanced and sophisticated mechanisms within cyber security that will enable effective responses to sophisticated cybercrime attacks. The research paper examines the current studies of AI-driven cybersecurity methods and outlines the use of AI techniques in various cybersecurity challenges.


### Key Findings

* Artificial Intelligence has become increasingly important in addressing complex cybersecurity threats.
* AI can support more intelligent cybersecurity defence mechanisms.
* Intelligent cybersecurity systems can assist with making decisions when responding to sophisticated attacks.
* Different Artificial Intelligence techniques have been investigated across multiple cybersecurity areas.
* Existing research demonstrates significant interest in using AI to strengthen cybersecurity.

### Methodology

* Systematic mapping study of existing literature related to Artificial Intelligence applications in cybersecurity.
* Existing studies were identified, classified, and analysed to understand major AI techniques and cybersecurity research areas.

### Contribution

Provides a structured overview of existing research on Artificial Intelligence in cybersecurity and helps identify the main areas where AI has been applied to combat cybercrime.

### Research Gap

* There is a need for further research into intelligent methods capable of responding to increasingly sophisticated cyber attacks.
* Existing AI cybersecurity research is distributed across different techniques and application areas.
* Further development of robust and intelligent cybersecurity mechanisms is required.
* Additional research is needed to understand how AI techniques can be effectively implemented in practical cybersecurity environments.

# Common Themes

* Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning improve cybersecurity through intelligent and automated threat detection.
* ML and DL techniques are widely applied to intrusion detection and other cybersecurity problems.
* AI-based approaches can identify complex patterns and suspicious network activities.
* Cybersecurity datasets play an important role in the effectiveness of Machine Learning models.
* AI and Machine Learning provide advantages for responding to increasingly complex and evolving cyber threats.
* Intelligent cybersecurity systems can improve traditional security mechanisms.

---

# Research Gaps

The three papers identify several common research gaps:

* Improving the trustworthiness of AI and Machine Learning cybersecurity systems.
* Developing stronger protection against adversarial attacks targeting ML models.
* Improving the quality and relevance of cybersecurity datasets.
* Developing AI models that can adapt to continuously evolving cyber threats.
* Improving the reliability and robustness of AI-based threat detection.
* Increasing the practical implementation of AI-based cybersecurity solutions in real-world environments.

---

# Relevance to Our Research

These three papers directly support our research question:

**Research Question**

> How do Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning improve cybersecurity by enhancing threat detection and mitigating cyber attacks?

AI, ML and DL can enhance cybersecurity in various ways such as assisting in intrusion detection, recognising suspicious patterns, analysing vast cybersecurity data, and aiding in intelligent cybersecurity responses, as shown by the existing literature. The papers also show that, though AI for cyber security is important, it has drawbacks, including in regard to adversarial attacks, quality of datasets, fast changing threats and the reliability of Machine Learning models. These challenges are, therefore, crucial to enhancing the usefulness of AI-powered cybersecurity solutions in real-world applications.
