# Research Topic: Impact of Artificial Intelligence on Cybersecurity

## 1. Research Question

**Impact of Artificial Intelligence on Cybersecurity:**  
Investigate how Artificial Intelligence (AI) and Machine Learning (ML) are used to enhance cybersecurity measures, detect threats, and mitigate cyberattacks.

## 2. Hypothesis

Organizations that implement AI- and ML-based cybersecurity systems will detect cyber threats more accurately, respond to incidents faster, and experience fewer successful cyberattacks than organizations relying solely on traditional cybersecurity methods.

## 3. Evaluation

The research question and hypothesis are effective because they are:

- **Clear:** They define the focus on AI and ML in cybersecurity.
- **Focused:** They concentrate on threat detection, incident response, and attack mitigation.
- **Relevant:** They address a significant and current issue in cybersecurity.
- **Measurable:** The outcomes can be evaluated using metrics such as detection accuracy, response time, and the number of successful cyberattacks.
- **Testable:** The hypothesis can be tested by comparing organizations that use AI/ML-based cybersecurity systems with those that rely on traditional methods.

Overall, they provide a strong foundation for conducting research on the impact of AI and ML in improving cybersecurity.
