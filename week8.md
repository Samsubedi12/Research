# Survey Design – Impact of Artificial Intelligence on Cybersecurity

## 1. Objectives of the Survey

The primary objective of this survey is to investigate people's understanding and perceptions of Artificial Intelligence (AI) and Machine Learning (ML) in cybersecurity. The survey aims to collect information about participants' awareness of AI-based cybersecurity technologies and their opinions about the effectiveness of AI in detecting and preventing cyber threats.

The specific objectives of the survey are to:

* Determine the level of participants' awareness of AI and ML in cybersecurity.
* Identify how participants perceive the effectiveness of AI in detecting cyber threats.
* Examine whether participants believe AI can improve the prevention of cyberattacks.
* Investigate participants' perceptions of AI's ability to reduce cybersecurity response time.
* Identify concerns about the use of AI in cybersecurity, including privacy, false positives, and security risks.
* Determine whether participants believe AI-based cybersecurity systems are more effective than traditional cybersecurity methods.
* Understand participants' overall attitudes toward the future use of AI in cybersecurity.

These objectives are aligned with the overall research question: **“How do Artificial Intelligence (AI) and Machine Learning (ML) improve cybersecurity by enhancing threat detection, preventing cyberattacks, and reducing response times compared to traditional security methods?”**

## 2. How the Survey Will Be Conducted and Distributed

The survey will be conducted as an **online questionnaire** using a platform such as Google Forms. An online survey is suitable because it allows participants to complete the questionnaire conveniently using a computer or mobile device. It also makes the collection and organization of responses easier and allows the research group to export the results for analysis.

The survey link will be distributed to the target participants through university communication channels, class groups, email, and appropriate online student communities. Group members may also share the survey link with eligible participants through their personal networks.

Participation will be voluntary. Participants will receive a short introduction explaining the purpose of the research, the approximate time required to complete the survey, and that their responses will be used for academic research purposes.

The survey will remain open for an appropriate period, such as one to two weeks, to provide participants with sufficient time to respond.

## 3. Selected Sample for the Survey

The target population for the survey will consist primarily of **university students and people with a general interest or background in information technology and cybersecurity**. Participants may have different levels of knowledge about AI and cybersecurity, which will allow the research group to compare general perceptions with those of participants who have greater technical knowledge.

The expected sample size will be approximately **50–100 participants**. This sample size is manageable for a student research project while providing enough responses to identify general patterns and trends in participants' opinions.

The survey will use **convenience sampling**, where participants are recruited based on their accessibility to the research group. Although this method is practical and cost-effective, the sample may not represent the entire population. Therefore, the findings will be interpreted as an indication of the views of the surveyed participants rather than as a representation of all university students or cybersecurity professionals.

### Open-Ended Question

**Question 8: In your opinion, what are the main benefits or challenges of using Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning in cybersecurity?**

This question will allow participants to provide their own opinions and experiences that may not be captured by the structured response options.

### Survey Analysis

The responses to the close-ended questions will be analyzed quantitatively using frequencies, percentages, and graphs. For example, the research group can calculate the percentage of participants who agree that AI improves threat detection or reduces cybersecurity response times.

The open-ended responses will be analyzed qualitatively by identifying common themes and recurring opinions. For example, responses may be grouped into themes such as improved threat detection, automation, privacy concerns, false positives, cost, and dependence on AI.

The quantitative and qualitative findings will then be compared with the research question and hypothesis to determine whether the collected evidence supports or does not support the hypothesis.

## 5. Ethical Considerations

Several ethical considerations will be taken into account when conducting and distributing the survey.

First, participation will be **voluntary**. Participants will not be forced or pressured to complete the survey and will be informed that they can choose not to participate.

Second, **informed consent** will be obtained before participants begin the survey. The introduction will explain the purpose of the research, what participation involves, and how the collected information will be used.

Third, the survey will avoid collecting unnecessary **personally identifiable information** such as full names, passwords, or private contact information. Responses will be treated as confidential and used only for the purpose of the academic research project.

Participants will also be informed about how their responses will be stored and used. The research group will take reasonable measures to protect the collected data and restrict access to authorized group members.

The survey questions will be designed to avoid unnecessary sensitive or intrusive questions. Participants will also have the option to skip the survey if they do not wish to participate.

Finally, the results will be reported **honestly and objectively**. The research group will not manipulate, fabricate, or selectively remove responses to produce a preferred outcome. Any limitations of the sample or survey methodology will be acknowledged when interpreting the results.
